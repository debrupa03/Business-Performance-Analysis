Business Performance Analysis | Power BI & Excel MIS

*Project Overview*

This project analyzes business performance using Power BI and Excel MIS reports, focusing on sales, profitability, customer segments, regional performance, time-based trends, and exception monitoring.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end reporting workflow, from data preparation and modelling to KPI development, visualization, performance reporting, and identification of areas requiring further investigation.

*Objectives*

Analyze sales and profitability across regions, customer segments, and product categories.

Monitor business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).

Evaluate year-over-year growth and year-to-date sales performance.

Identify loss-making transactions and potential profitability concerns.

Develop structured Excel MIS reports for performance monitoring.

*Tools and Technologies*

Power BI: Interactive reports and business performance visualization

DAX: KPI measures, time intelligence, and profitability analysis

Power Query: Data preparation and transformation

Microsoft Excel: XLOOKUP, PivotTables, reporting, and exception analysis

*Project Components*

1. Power BI Report

The four-page Power BI report covers:

Overall business performance

Regional and customer segment analysis

Time intelligence and sales growth

Profitability diagnostics and root-cause exploration

The data model uses a central order fact table, supporting dimension tables, and a calendar table to organize business data and support analytical reporting.

2. Excel MIS Reports

Four structured MIS reports were developed:

Region Performance: Compares sales and profitability across regions.

Segment Performance: Evaluates performance across customer segments.

Product Performance: Analyzes product-level sales, profit, and margins.

Exception Report: Flags loss-making transactions, high discounts, low profit margins, and unusually high shipping costs for further review.

*Key Findings*

Technology was the leading category by sales, contributing 37.53%.

The Consumer segment generated approximately $6.51 million in sales, representing the largest customer segment.

The Central region recorded the highest sales, at approximately $2.86 million.

The Copiers sub-category generated approximately $258.57K in profit.

The Tables sub-category recorded a loss of approximately $64.08K.

Loss-making transactions accounted for approximately $0.92 million in aggregate negative profit.

These findings highlight areas of strong performance and potential profitability concerns that may warrant further business investigation.
